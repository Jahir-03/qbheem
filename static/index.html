Key generation efficiency

As channel noise rises, error correction leaks more bits and privacy amplification removes more, until nothing safe is left.

Partial eavesdropping

A light tap can slip under the 11% limit. The finite-key bound still refuses to produce a key when the estimated leakage is too high.

RSA-2048 key exchange

Bob encrypts a session key to Alice's public key. In "man in the middle" mode, Eve swaps the public key in transit.

Record now, break later

Eve stores the encrypted exchange. Here a toy 64-bit RSA modulus is factored on a laptop. Against RSA-2048 the same role is played by Shor's algorithm on a large quantum computer.
